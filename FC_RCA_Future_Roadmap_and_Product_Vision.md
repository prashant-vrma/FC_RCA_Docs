# 1. Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision and strategic roadmap for the Forecast Adherence RCA Agent.

The objective is to evolve the solution from an AI-assisted Root Cause Analysis application into an enterprise AI Decision Intelligence Platform for Workforce Management.

The roadmap outlines future capabilities, technology evolution, AI maturity, and business expansion opportunities.


# 2. Product Vision

To build the industry's most trusted AI-powered Workforce Forecast Intelligence Platform that enables organizations to proactively predict forecast risks, explain business outcomes, recommend corrective actions, and continuously learn from organizational knowledge.


# 3. Product Mission

The platform should enable organizations to:

- Detect forecast risks early.
- Reduce manual RCA effort.
- Improve forecasting accuracy.
- Accelerate decision-making.
- Capture organizational knowledge.
- Continuously improve through AI-assisted learning.


# 4. Strategic Goals

The long-term strategy focuses on:

- AI-first operations
- Explainable decision intelligence
- Enterprise scalability
- Human-AI collaboration
- Continuous learning
- Predictive analytics
- Autonomous recommendations
