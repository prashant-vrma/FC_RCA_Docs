# RCA_Project_Governance_and_Operating_Model

## Forecast Adherence RCA Agent

**Version:** 1.0  
**Document Type:** Governance Framework and Operating Model  
**Domain:** Workforce Management (WFM) / Workforce Optimization (WFO)


# 1. Purpose

This document defines the governance structure, operating model, decision-making framework, ownership model, and lifecycle management approach for the Forecast Adherence RCA Agent.

The objective is to ensure the solution remains:

- Business aligned
- Secure
- Governed
- Scalable
- Continuously improving
- Operationally sustainable


# 2. Governance Objectives

The governance framework ensures:

- Business ownership of RCA decisions
- Responsible AI usage
- High-quality analytical outputs
- Controlled changes
- Standardized operating procedures
- Regulatory and enterprise compliance


# 3. Governance Principles

The RCA Agent shall operate using the following principles:

## Business First

Business objectives always take precedence over technology decisions.

## Human Accountability

AI supports decisions.

Humans remain accountable for operational decisions.

## Explainable AI

Every recommendation must be explainable and evidence-based.

## Controlled Change

All production changes must follow an approved change management process.

## Continuous Improvement

Feedback from users should continuously improve the platform.

# 10. AI Governance


The AI governance framework should monitor:

- Output quality
- Hallucination rate
- Recommendation usefulness
- User acceptance
- Confidence distribution


AI-generated recommendations should never bypass business validation.


# 11. Data Governance


The data governance framework should ensure:

- Data quality
- Data completeness
- Data lineage
- Metadata management
- Data ownership
- Data retention compliance


# 12. Knowledge Governance


Knowledge should be:

- Reviewed
- Validated
- Approved
- Version controlled
- Periodically reviewed


Only approved knowledge should be available to the production AI Agent.


# 13. Risk Management


Key governance risks include:

## Poor Data Quality

Mitigation:

Automated validation and business review.


## AI Hallucinations

Mitigation:

Evidence-based reasoning and human validation.


## Low User Adoption

Mitigation:

Training, usability improvements, and business engagement.


## Model Performance Degradation

Mitigation:

Continuous monitoring and periodic evaluation.
