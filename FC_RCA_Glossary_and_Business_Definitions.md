# 1. Purpose

This document defines the standard business terminology, KPI definitions, forecasting concepts, AI terminology, and operational vocabulary used throughout the Forecast Adherence RCA Agent solution.

The objective is to establish a common language for business users, analysts, developers, architects, and AI models to ensure consistent understanding and interpretation.


# 2. Forecasting Terminology


## Forecast

The predicted contact volume expected for a future time period.


## Actual Offered

The actual number of customer contacts received during the selected period.


## Forecast Variance

Measures both the magnitude and direction of forecast error.

Formula:

Forecast Variance % = (Actual Offered - Forecast) / Forecast


Interpretation:

Positive Value

Actual demand exceeded forecast.

Direction:

Under Forecast


Negative Value

Forecast exceeded actual demand.

Direction:

Over Forecast


Zero

Forecast exactly matched actual demand.


## Forecast Adherence

Measures forecast accuracy irrespective of direction.

Formula:

Forecast Adherence % = 1 - ABS((Actual Offered - Forecast) / Forecast)


Important:

Forecast Adherence indicates only the magnitude of forecast accuracy.

It must never be used to determine whether a forecast was over or under.

Forecast direction must always be determined using Forecast Variance.


## Forecast Bias

The tendency to consistently over-forecast or under-forecast over multiple periods.


## Forecast Accuracy

A general measure indicating how closely forecast values align with actual outcomes.


## Forecast Horizon

The future period covered by a forecast.

Examples:

- Daily
- Weekly
- Monthly
- Quarterly


# 3. Workforce Management Terminology


## Queue

A logical grouping of customer contacts managed together.


## Business Segment

A business area or operational group for which forecasting and planning are performed.


## Contact Volume

The number of customer interactions received.


## Demand Driver

A factor influencing customer demand.

Examples:

- Product launches
- Warranty expirations
- Marketing campaigns
- Holidays
- Seasonality


## Capacity Planning

The process of determining required staffing levels to meet forecasted demand.


## Schedule Compliance

The extent to which employees follow planned schedules.


## Real-Time Management (RTA)

Operational management of staffing and service performance during live operations.


# 4. Root Cause Analysis Terminology


## Root Cause

The primary reason responsible for a forecast miss.


## Contributing Factor

A secondary factor influencing the forecast outcome.


## Corrective Action

An activity intended to reduce the likelihood of future forecast misses.


## Recommendation

A proposed action generated by the AI Agent based on analytical findings.


## Evidence

Verified analytical or business information supporting a root cause.


## Confidence Level

The AI's assessment of certainty regarding the identified root cause.

Typical values:

- High
- Medium
- Low


# 5. AI Terminology


## Large Language Model (LLM)

A generative AI model capable of understanding and generating natural language.


## Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)

A technique where relevant business knowledge is retrieved and supplied to the AI model before generating a response.


## Prompt

Instructions provided to the AI model describing the task to perform.


## Prompt Engineering

The process of designing prompts that improve AI output quality.


## Hallucination

An AI-generated statement that is unsupported by available evidence.


## Embedding

A numerical representation of text used to identify semantic similarity.


## Vector Database

A specialized database that stores embeddings for semantic search and knowledge retrieval.


## Knowledge Base

A repository containing validated business knowledge used during RCA generation.

# 11. Standard Formula Reference


Forecast Variance %

= (Actual Offered - Forecast) / Forecast


Forecast Adherence %

= 1 - ABS((Actual Offered - Forecast) / Forecast)


Forecast Error

= Actual Offered - Forecast


Forecast Error %

= ((Actual Offered - Forecast) / Forecast) × 100


# 12. Business Rules


Rule 1

Forecast Variance determines forecast direction.


Rule 2

Forecast Adherence measures only forecast accuracy magnitude.


Rule 3

ABS() removes direction information.


Rule 4

Root causes must always be supported by evidence.


Rule 5

AI recommendations must align with identified root causes.


Rule 6

Human approval is required before production use of AI-generated RCA.
